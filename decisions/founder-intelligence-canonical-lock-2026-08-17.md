# Founder Intelligence OS — Canonical Architecture Lock

This decision resolves architectural drift accumulated during the 2026-08-17 Founder Command Center / Founder Intelligence discussion. Earlier explorations remain useful history, but this file is the canonical decision state unless new real-world evidence explicitly reopens it.

## PRODUCT_SOUL — LOCKED

**Founder Intelligence helps a company remember how it got here, understand where it is now, and choose the best available path to where it wants to go.**

Product law:

> Complex company underneath. Radical clarity on top.

Operating law:

> Blocked does not mean stopped.

Priority law:

> Every important action must answer: toward what outcome?

Temporal model:

> PAST -> PRESENT -> FUTURE

- Past: Replay what was known, decided, done, spent, and learned.
- Present: Orient founders to truth, blockers, money, responsibilities, and next action.
- Future: Pre-live alternative routes and scenarios without presenting simulation as fact.

## CORE_ARCHITECTURE — LOCKED

### Human experience layer

1. **ROOM** — shared founder truth: goal, health, money, three critical missions, blocker, decision, meaningful changes, impact.
2. **MAP** — visual time-aware Outcome / Action Branch Graph showing paths, dependencies, owners, money, blockers, alternatives, and consequences.
3. **FOCUS** — individual founder view with a maximum of three highest-leverage actions and the reason each matters.
4. **ASK** — Founder AI conversational control layer over approved company context.

These are cognitive states, not an obligation to create four rigid pages forever.

### Intelligence layer

- Outcome Intelligence
- Detour Intelligence
- Financial / Capital Intelligence
- Decision Intelligence
- Risk Intelligence
- Capacity Intelligence (later user-facing)
- Capability / Skill Intelligence (later user-facing)

### Memory / graph layer

One time-aware company graph and event ledger connecting:

- business unit;
- goal / objective;
- outcome / metric;
- driver;
- branch / path;
- task / action;
- blocker / dependency;
- person;
- AI agent / automation;
- decision;
- meeting;
- assumption;
- evidence;
- money / capital event;
- result;
- learning;
- document / source;
- customer / lead where relevant;
- impact outcome.

Every material node or decision preserves the information state that existed at the time, including source, timestamp, confidence, alternatives considered, expected result, actual result, and retrospective learning. Never rewrite the historical belief using hindsight.

### Operations / source layer

Integrate existing systems before replacing them:

- calendar / scheduling;
- meeting capture such as Fireflies;
- task/project systems;
- CRM / pipeline;
- finance sources;
- GitHub;
- Google Drive / documents;
- communication such as WhatsApp Business, Slack, Teams, Google Chat, or email where technically and legally appropriate.

## SIGNATURE_MECHANICS — LOCKED

### 1. Visual Action Branches

The MAP visibly connects action -> dependency -> driver -> business outcome. Founders navigate branches rather than read disconnected task lists.

### 2. Detour Engine

When a route becomes blocked, Founder Intelligence identifies other executable routes toward the same outcome and explains tradeoffs in time, cost, evidence, capacity, risk, and expected contribution.

### 3. Living Company Timeline / Replay

Founders can travel backward through the graph to see what was known, what was believed, which options existed, what decision was chosen, what money moved, and what actually happened. Historical fact must remain separate from counterfactual simulation.

### 4. Pre-Live / What-If

Founders can simulate alternative future branches using explicit assumptions and base/upside/downside logic. Simulated outcomes are never shown as recorded history or guaranteed probability.

### 5. Founder Catch-Up / Since You Left

Compress meaningful organizational change into a short brief and end with the action or decision that most requires the founder.

### 6. Explainability / Why

Every consequential recommendation, metric, or projection can expose source, freshness, assumptions, confidence, and reasoning.

### 7. Meaningful visual state change

Completing real work changes the graph: branches activate, dependencies unlock, blockers disappear, paths become available, and evidence updates. Do not use meaningless XP as the primary motivational mechanic.

## V0_BUILD — LOCKED

The LastBench pilot must test the core behavioral loop, not the complete platform.

Build / prototype only:

1. **ROOM** with real LastBench goal, real capital truth, max three founder missions, one critical blocker / decision, and mission / impact kept subtle.
2. **MAP** with one real LastBench objective and interactive Visual Action Branches.
3. **Detour** for a blocked branch and alternative routes.
4. **FOCUS** for Erfan, Sayem, and Fahim with max three missions each.
5. **ASK** limited initially to: what matters, what changed, what is blocked, what can we do instead, and why.
6. **Decision / event ledger** from day one: assumption -> decision -> action -> result -> learning.
7. **Money truth**: invested, spent, committed, available. No invented forecast.
8. **Meeting ingestion loop**: meeting -> decision -> task -> owner -> graph.
9. **Since You Left** using meaningful changes only.
10. **Provenance / freshness / confidence** for important insights and metrics.
11. **Historical state capture from day one**, even if the beautiful Replay interface comes slightly after initial pilot UI.

The V0 success question is not whether the dashboard looks impressive. It is whether founders make better / faster decisions and voluntarily return to the system.

## ARCHITECT_NOW_UI_LATER — LOCKED

Support these entities / boundaries in the underlying model now, but do not expose full feature sets in V0:

- multiple business units: Malaysia / LastBench, Class A, Co.lab;
- people and role / ownership relationships;
- AI agents and automation as execution resources;
- capabilities / skills;
- capital allocation by business unit;
- impact outcomes;
- historical graph / replay states;
- future scenario states;
- privacy boundary between Erfan Second Brain and company-safe context.

## DEFERRED — LOCKED

- Capacity Intelligence UI: intern vs freelancer vs hire vs agent recommendations.
- Class A contextual Skill Quests.
- advanced financial forecasting and calibrated probability.
- full organization / capability visualization.
- external-client templates and multi-company tenancy.
- native communication threads only if integrations later prove insufficient.
- native document authoring only if integrations later prove insufficient.
- richer scenario simulation after real historical data exists.

Deferred means strategically retained but deliberately not built now.

## KILLED — LOCKED

- giant 20-tab dashboard;
- fake financial or success metrics;
- generic Slack clone;
- generic Notion clone;
- native video-meeting product without evidence of need;
- XP farming / meaningless badges;
- founder or employee leaderboards as primary motivation;
- employee productivity scores;
- autonomous firing / hiring decisions;
- autonomous consequential spending or commitments without approval;
- constant trend/news feed;
- probability claims without defensible calibration;
- positioning as "all-in-one collaboration software."

### Reopen triggers

A killed feature may be reopened only when repeated observed user behavior shows that integrations or retained mechanisms cannot solve the underlying problem, or when new evidence materially changes the economics / trust / adoption tradeoff.

## DR.X / ERFAN SECOND BRAIN BOUNDARY — LOCKED

Reuse Second Brain methods and approved context, not private memory indiscriminately.

Reusable infrastructure:

- provenance-aware retrieval;
- MemPalace semantic recall;
- Graphify / relationship intelligence where useful;
- Decision Council;
- learning ledger;
- confidence / freshness discipline;
- context-aware communication and approval gates.

Private Erfan information remains outside shared company truth unless explicitly approved and relevant.

## COMMERCIAL PATH — PROVISIONAL BUT PREFERRED

1. LastBench dogfood.
2. Prove repeated founder behavior and decision value.
3. Use Founder Intelligence as a Co.lab methodology / service-assisted installation.
4. Run 3–5 external founder-team pilots.
5. Prove willingness to pay and continued use without Co.lab manually holding the system together.
6. Productize repeated patterns into SaaS only after evidence.

This path remains provisional because external willingness to pay and retention are not yet observed.

## UNRESOLVED HYPOTHESES

- Will founders understand the Visual Action Branch model faster than conventional project / dashboard views?
- Will Detour recommendations change real decisions rather than merely state obvious alternatives?
- Can enough operating context be captured automatically to prevent maintenance fatigue?
- Will historical decision / outcome memory materially improve recommendations over time?
- Will external founder teams pay for this level of clarity and intelligence?
- Is Bangladesh the best commercial beachhead, or mainly the first laboratory?

## BUILD-FINAL STATUS

The architecture is `BUILD-FINAL` for the LastBench experiment.

New product ideas must be classified through `drx-architecture-convergence` as REINFORCES, CLARIFIES, EXTENDS, CONTRADICTS, or REPLACES. No future simulation or audit may silently mutate this architecture.

Further quality gains should now come primarily from prototype behavior and observed evidence, not continued feature ideation.
