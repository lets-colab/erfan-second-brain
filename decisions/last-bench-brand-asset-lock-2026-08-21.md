# Last Bench Canonical Brand Asset Lock — 2026-08-21

## Decision

For every Last Bench artifact, approved logos, brand marks, and supplied real-person portraits are protected canonical assets. They must never be recreated, redrawn, approximated, traced, substituted, or regenerated by an image model when the brief requires the actual approved asset/person.

This decision exists because the same logo-regeneration failure recurred across multiple Last Bench poster attempts despite prior correction.

## Canonical Last Bench logo assets currently approved

Persistent ChatGPT Library assets:
- `master-logo-light.png` — use on light/white backgrounds; black Last Bench wordmark + green bench/rising-arrow mark on transparent canvas.
- `master-logo-dark.png` — use on dark/black backgrounds; white Last Bench wordmark + green bench/rising-arrow mark on transparent canvas.

The internal logo geometry is protected. The correct Last Bench mark includes the detailed bench construction and the specific rising-arrow/path geometry. Do not simplify it into generic horizontal bars plus an arrow.

If another later asset is explicitly approved by Erfan/Dr.X as the replacement master, that later explicit approval supersedes these filenames. Until then, these are the canonical variants.

## Incident diagnosis — 21 August 2026 agent poster

The generated agent-recognition poster failed brand integrity because the logo in the poster was not the canonical Last Bench logo. The image-generation path redrew the mark rather than compositing the approved source asset.

Observed mismatch against `master-logo-light.png`:
- bench geometry was simplified;
- the original bench legs/slats/structural relationships were not preserved;
- the arrow/path geometry was changed;
- the fine trailing line/dot detail of the canonical mark was lost;
- the wordmark was re-rendered rather than placed as the approved source.

The supplied consultant portrait was also routed through full-image generation, so the face/likeness was recreated rather than guaranteeing faithful preservation of the supplied photograph. For a recognition post, that is an identity-integrity failure even if the generated person looks similar.

This is a critical brand/identity failure even if the replacement looks visually polished.

## Root cause

Failure class: **tooling + verification + requirement isolation**.

1. The full poster was sent through a generative image renderer, including the protected logo and portrait layers.
2. The logo was treated as a visual concept rather than an immutable source asset.
3. The consultant's real supplied portrait was treated as generative scene content instead of a protected identity asset.
4. Final QC checked general appearance but did not perform a source-vs-render brand/portrait comparison.
5. A separate campaign message was allowed to bleed into the recognition poster: the friends-and-family/AI-skills CTA belongs to a different artifact unless explicitly requested for the partner-recognition creative.
6. A generated QR was included without a verified approved registration destination, which is not acceptable for a functional QR.

## Permanent production rule

Use a two-layer workflow for Last Bench creative:

`GENERATIVE VISUAL LAYER -> DETERMINISTIC BRAND/IDENTITY/TEXT/QR LAYER -> FINAL SOURCE COMPARISON`

### Generative layer may create
- background imagery;
- environment;
- decorative shapes and non-critical illustration;
- fictional people only when requested.

### Deterministic protected layer must contain
- exact `master-logo-light.png` or `master-logo-dark.png`;
- exact supplied consultant/person portrait when likeness accuracy matters;
- exact names/titles/dates;
- contact details;
- campaign CTA;
- any functional QR generated from the approved URL;
- other locked Last Bench marks.

Protected real-person photos may be cropped, masked, background-removed, or color-adjusted non-destructively when needed, but the face/identity must not be reconstructed by generation unless Erfan explicitly asks for a stylized transformation.

The protected layer must be composited after generation using deterministic layout/editing. The logo and required real-person portrait must never be regenerated inside the generative step.

## Campaign isolation for 24 August

Keep these assets distinct unless Erfan explicitly merges them:

1. **Partner recognition post** — recognizes/welcomes a specific education consultant/partner. Primary purpose: status, recognition, credibility, social proof.
2. **Partner invitation/recruitment post** — asks relevant education consultants/agents to register/attend.

4. **Student/application post** — targets students/parents for study opportunities and application support.

Do not move the AI-skills CTA from the friends-and-family campaign into a partner-recognition poster unless explicitly requested.

## Mandatory acceptance test before handoff

A Last Bench branded artifact is not ready until:
- canonical source asset is retrieved;
- correct light/dark logo variant selected;
- logo is placed deterministically, never generated;
- supplied real-person portrait is preserved when likeness accuracy is required;
- aspect ratio and internal geometry are preserved;
- final render is visually compared to the canonical master and supplied portrait;
- names, titles, event date/day, address and contacts are checked;
- campaign purpose/audience is not mixed with another asset;
- any QR decodes from the final exported artifact to the approved destination;
- final artifact itself is inspected after the last edit.

## Escalation rule

Because this failure has already repeated, any future Last Bench logo or supplied-portrait mismatch is a **process regression**. Stop the creative workflow immediately and repair the production pipeline before producing another variant.
