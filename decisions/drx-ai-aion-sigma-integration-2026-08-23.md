# DR.X AI — AION SIGMA Integration Decision

## Decision

Integrate the 2026-08-23 “strongest-designed AI” conversation into the Erfan Second Brain as a durable DR.X AI architecture standard under the internal codename **AION SIGMA**.

AION SIGMA is not a separate product and not a claim that DR.X has trained a frontier foundation model. It is the target compound-intelligence design standard for DR.X AI.

Canonical architecture source:

`architecture/DRX-AION-SIGMA.md`

## What was requested

Erfan asked, before the EKUSH discussion, how an AI system should be built if the design goal were to compete for the strongest overall AI capability in the world. The conversation converged on a compound system rather than one chatbot or one fixed model: multimodal specialist intelligence, persistent context/memory, model routing, deep reasoning, a council, tools/agents/code, adversarial critique, verification, a verdict layer, execution, and learning from observed outcomes.

Erfan later explicitly instructed that this architecture be integrated into the Erfan Second Brain for **DR.X AI** and saved durably.

Evidence class: `DIRECT_USER_STATEMENT` for the request and integration instruction. Specific software decomposition and quality standards are `DERIVED_INFERENCE` / architecture synthesis unless already present in canonical DR.X files.

## Locked interpretation

Use the phrase:

> **Strongest-designed, pending proof.**

Meaning:
- ambition may be maximal;
- architecture may be designed to compete at frontier quality;
- no “world's strongest” claim is permitted without independent, repeatable benchmark and real-world evidence.

## Relationship to existing architecture

This decision **extends, not replaces**, `architecture/DRX-AI-OS.md`.

Existing rules remain authoritative for:
- canonical source ownership;
- runtime control plane;
- governance and least privilege;
- observability;
- memory provenance;
- acceptance tests;
- execution QC.

AION SIGMA adds an explicit target for:
- model-agnostic routing;
- compound specialist intelligence;
- multimodal orchestration;
- deep reasoning / Fable-2036;
- independent Decision Council review when justified;
- adversarial red-team passes;
- verification-before-verdict;
- observed-outcome learning.

## Naming

- **DR.X AI** remains the system/product identity.
- **AION SIGMA** is an internal compound-intelligence architecture codename/standard.
- Do not present AION SIGMA as a separately trained foundation model unless that becomes fact in the future.

## Durable lesson

Do not confuse **base-model intelligence** with **system intelligence**. DR.X AI should seek the highest verified outcome quality by combining the best current engines with durable context, routing, specialist skills, tools, execution, adversarial review, verification, and measured learning.

The engine portfolio may change. The higher-level contracts should remain stable unless evidence justifies a redesign.
