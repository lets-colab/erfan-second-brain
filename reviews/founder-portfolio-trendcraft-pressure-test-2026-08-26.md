# Founder Portfolio Trendcraft — Pressure Test

## User requirement

The requested capability was not merely to reproduce current premium founder/portfolio aesthetics. The design system must be capable of generating outcomes with enough originality, usefulness and ownability to plausibly influence future design practice.

This review applies the repository's Fable reasoning discipline and execution truth boundary. `Trend-capable` is treated as a design hypothesis. `Created a trend` requires external evidence and cannot be declared from internal judgment alone.

## Fresh 2026 design context

A current scan of 2026 web-design discourse reinforces three relevant conditions:

1. AI-assisted creation raises the visual baseline and increases the risk of algorithmic sameness.
2. Proprietary effects and ownable visual systems are becoming more important than access to fashionable techniques.
3. Human craft, deliberate editorial direction, interaction design and distinctive visual language are increasingly used to signal authorship and quality.

Supporting public reference used during the pressure test:
- Webflow, `8 web design trends to watch in 2026`: https://webflow.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026
- Webflow, `The 2026 State of the Website`: https://webflow.com/resources/report/2026-state-of-the-website
- Current Awwwards portfolio/design examples were reviewed as supporting creative context, not as templates to imitate.

## Pre-repair verdict — FAIL

The earlier proposed founder-site direction had strong taste and correctly emphasized narrative UX, editorial art direction, cinematic media, typography, motion, interaction, proof architecture and restraint.

However, it still failed the higher `trend-creation` requirement because it could optimize and combine existing premium design patterns without a formal mechanism for originating a new design grammar.

Critical gaps:

- no required cultural-tension derivation;
- no mandatory 10-direction divergence before convergence;
- no distinction between `saturated`, `emerging`, `underused` and `white-space` patterns;
- no signature-primitive ownership test;
- no anti-template distance test;
- no test for whether another designer could derive a broader principle from the system;
- no explicit protection against confusing Awwwards-style sophistication with originality;
- no requirement that the central idea survive static, mobile and reduced-motion states;
- no cross-founder benchmark proving the method changes when founder truth changes;
- no truth rule preventing trend-seeking from manufacturing founder mythology.

## Repairs introduced

The installed `drx-founder-portfolio-trendcraft` v1.0.0 adds:

1. **Founder Truth Compiler** — prevents invented biography, achievements and founder mythology.
2. **Trend & Saturation Scan** — separates saturated fashion from genuine white space.
3. **Cultural Tension Engine** — new design ideas begin from a real social/brand tension rather than a visual-style menu.
4. **10 Divergent Trend Hypotheses** — convergence is forbidden before ten materially distinct systems exist.
5. **Signature Primitive Test** — each surviving concept requires one useful, memorable, repeatable founder-specific interaction/visual law.
6. **Anti-Template Distance Test** — rejects concepts that can be recreated through font/color/photo swaps or applied to unrelated founders unchanged.
7. **Adoption / Trend Potential Test** — evaluates memorability, ownability, utility, repeatability, derivative potential, cultural resonance, craft visibility and longevity.
8. **Mobile / Reduced-Motion Survival** — meaning must survive without desktop-only spectacle.
9. **Conversion Without Corporate UI** — originality cannot destroy trust or business usefulness.
10. **10-Case Benchmark Suite** — tests product, education, finance, creator, polymath, low-proof, no-portrait, low-bandwidth, local-to-global and regulated founder classes.

## Ten-case pressure-test result

The repaired contract passes the benchmark methodology because each founder class forces changes across:

- narrative architecture;
- proof strategy;
- signature primitive;
- visual grammar;
- interaction grammar;
- motion intensity;
- mobile behavior;
- trust/conversion pattern.

The skill explicitly fails any attempt to reuse one black/white/editorial/WebGL aesthetic across all ten cases.

**Post-repair contract verdict: PASS.**

## What this PASS does not prove

It does not prove:

- a production website has been created;
- users prefer the output;
- conversion improves;
- the method wins awards;
- another designer will imitate it;
- the result creates an actual market trend;
- quality is repeatable across five or more real projects.

Those claims require real artifact and adoption evidence.

## Next evidence gate

Use the Sayem Ahmed founder portfolio as the first real benchmark run:

1. retrieve only verified founder evidence;
2. perform a fresh saturation scan;
3. generate ten divergent trend hypotheses;
4. kill seven explicitly;
5. concept the top three;
6. select one signature system;
7. create the full-page visual concept before implementation;
8. test desktop, mobile and reduced-motion behavior;
9. implement only after concept approval;
10. compare final render against the accepted concept and record user acceptance/corrections.

This first real artifact should be logged in `evaluations/skill-fitness.yaml`. It will count as one representative production run, not proof of repeatability by itself.
