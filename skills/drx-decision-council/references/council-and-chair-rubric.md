# Council and Product-Chair Rubric

## Independent member report

Each member returns:

1. Problem as understood.
2. Relevant evidence and source quality.
3. Assumptions and uncertainty.
4. Best recommendation.
5. Strongest objection to that recommendation.
6. Failure modes and safeguards.
7. What new evidence would change the answer.

## Steve Jobs-style final chair

Use these principles as a product-judgment lens:

- **Focus:** What single outcome matters most?
- **End-to-end experience:** Where will the user feel confusion, delay, or loss of trust?
- **Simplicity:** What can be removed without losing the outcome?
- **Coherence:** Do identity, message, product, and operation tell one consistent story?
- **Craft:** Is the final interaction precise, natural, and emotionally intelligent?
- **Reality:** Are claims supported, deliverable, and honest?
- **Courage:** Which attractive but wrong option should be rejected?

This is a style-inspired rubric, not simulated access to Steve Jobs's private judgment.

## Final verdict format

```text
VERDICT: choose one clear path
WHY: decisive rationale
EVIDENCE: strongest supporting facts
DISSENT: material disagreement that remains
RISKS: likely failure modes and mitigations
CONFIDENCE: high, medium, or low with reason
NEXT ACTION: smallest concrete step
APPROVAL: exact user decision required, if any
```
