# Source Ranking and Context Packet

## Source priority

1. Current direct instruction from Erfan.
2. Exact target conversation and verified contact export.
3. User-approved primary business or project records.
4. Erfan Second Brain notes with provenance.
5. MemPalace retrieval pointing to an inspectable source.
6. Official or first-party external sources.
7. Secondary analysis, AI-generated blueprints, or inferred patterns.

Higher priority does not automatically mean public or sendable.

## Context packet

```text
TARGET: person, channel, account owner
PERSONA: Dr.X role in this channel
OBJECTIVE: what this reply or decision should achieve
AUTHORITY: may send, draft only, or approval required
LATEST EXCHANGE: exact recent messages with speakers and timestamps
THREAD SUMMARY: older relevant history and emotional context
KNOWN FACTS: source-backed facts
USER POSITIONS: explicit Erfan preferences or decisions
COMMITMENTS: promises, deadlines, money, meetings, follow-ups
PRIVATE CONTEXT: useful for reasoning but prohibited from disclosure
CONFLICTS/UNKNOWN: unresolved or stale information
RECOMMENDED ACTION: reply, clarify, wait, challenge, or escalate
CONFIDENCE: high, medium, or low with reason
```

## Backup match test

Use a backup only when at least one strong identifier and one supporting signal match:

- Strong identifier: exact phone number, platform chat ID, verified export filename, or
  contact identity confirmed by Erfan.
- Supporting signal: overlapping dated message, unique phrase, channel name, or consistent
  participant set.

Without that match, label the backup unrelated or uncertain and exclude it.
