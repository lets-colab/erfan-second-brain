# Public-Facing Business Offer Playbook

## Conversation motion

When a contact asks what Erfan is building, what a business does, or whether it can help:

1. Understand the contact's role, stage, and problem from the thread.
2. Name the most relevant business or service in one sentence.
3. Explain the outcome for that person, not only the features.
4. Ask one useful qualifying question.
5. Offer one low-friction next step: a short overview, diagnostic conversation, demo, or
   introduction. Do not book, price, or promise delivery without authority.

## Approved Co.lab public positioning

Source: user-approved Google Drive document `Co.Lab` accessed 2026-07-19.

Co.lab is a brand and business development firm that helps founders, leadership teams, and
operators turn unclear positioning into brand clarity, fragmented execution into scalable
systems, and passive audiences into active communities.

Its public method is:

`Clarity -> Systems -> Community`

Relevant service lanes:

- brand identity, positioning, and strategic clarity;
- execution systems and activation planning;
- community, creator partnerships, advocacy, and trust-driven growth;
- creator-powered campaigns when they genuinely fit the customer's problem.

Do not quote internal investment figures, projections, success probabilities, cashback rates,
or ROI claims without current validation and explicit approval.

Natural first response:

> Co.lab helps businesses get clear on what they stand for, build the systems to execute it,
> and create a community that drives sustainable growth. What are you trying to improve
> right now: your positioning, your execution, or customer/community growth?

## Approved Last Bench public positioning

Source: Google Drive `Last Bench Blueprint` accessed 2026-07-19. Treat launch status and
market claims as plan data until independently verified.

The Last Bench is being built as a student guidance and transparency platform for Bangladeshi
students, initially focused on study pathways involving Malaysia. Its planned public pillars are:

- visible application and visa-file progress tracking;
- AI-assisted, human-verified education guidance;
- practical skills and creator community;
- tutor and coaching-center mentorship and referral network.

Describe these as work in development unless the current source confirms launch. Never expose
student data, partner terms, legal analysis, deed issues, private finances, or unverified outcome
claims.

## Offer discipline

- Diagnose before pitching.
- Sell the outcome, not the tool list.
- Keep the first message short and conversational.
- Use only verified public facts.
- Do not pressure, fabricate scarcity, or claim proof that does not exist.
- If the contact is a weak fit, say so or ask a clarifying question.
