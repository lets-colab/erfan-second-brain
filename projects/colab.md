# Co.lab

## Public positioning

Source: user-approved Google Drive document `Co.Lab` accessed 2026-07-19.

Co.lab is a brand and business development firm that helps founders, leadership teams, and
operators transform unclear positioning into brand clarity, fragmented execution into scalable
systems, and passive audiences into active communities.

Public method:

`Clarity -> Systems -> Community`

## Public service lanes

- Brand identity, positioning, narrative, and strategic clarity.
- Execution systems and activation planning.
- Community, creator partnerships, advocacy, and trust-driven growth.
- Creator-powered campaigns where they fit the customer's actual problem.

## Product / tool hypotheses

### Founder Command Center + OS

Status: validation.

An outcome-driven operating layer for founders and small teams that connects goals, meetings,
calendars, communication, tasks, financial state, decisions, company memory, and community
impact into one live command center. The key question is not "what work exists?" but
**"Are we actually moving toward the goal, what is blocking us, and what should each person do next?"**

The first design partner is LastBench. The product should be dogfooded internally before being
positioned as standalone SaaS. It fits Co.lab's method directly:

- Clarity -> mission, goals, roadmap, financial reality, decisions.
- Systems -> tasks, meetings, calendar, integrations, operating cadence, AI briefings.
- Community -> collaboration, creator/ambassador systems, culture, and meaningful impact work.

Detailed product hypothesis: `projects/founder-command-center-os.md`.

### Related future tool family

- Presence / Personal Portfolio OS — identity, CV, proof, personal brand and digital presence.
- Creator / Influencer Collaboration OS — creator relationships, campaign workflow, attribution,
  deliverables, performance and payments.

These are related hypotheses, not yet validated commitments. They may eventually share a common
identity/relationship/work graph but should initially be tested as separate workflows.

## Conversation offer

When someone asks about Co.lab, diagnose their current problem, connect it to the smallest
relevant service, explain the benefit, ask one qualifying question, and offer a short diagnostic,
overview, or introduction. Do not invent prices, availability, proof, ROI, or delivery promises.
