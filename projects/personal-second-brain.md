# Personal Second Brain

## User-stated objective

Create a reusable personal knowledge system that can keep learning, support this chat
as a personal assistant, work with other AI systems, and report how its knowledge
readiness changes whenever new durable knowledge is gained.

## Success conditions

- Knowledge remains locally owned and readable as ordinary Markdown.
- Obsidian can open the folder directly as a vault.
- MemPalace can provide semantic recall over an approved subset.
- Graphify can map relationships and provenance over an approved subset.
- Other AI systems can read an intentionally shared export without receiving secrets.
- The assistant reports a before score, after score, delta, evidence, confidence, and
  remaining blind spot after meaningful knowledge updates.

## Current scope

- Personal identity and professional history, when supplied and approved.
- Communication preferences and approved writing examples.
- Active projects including Last Bench, DR.X, and approved lets-colab repositories.
- Decisions, goals, operating rules, relationships, and project continuity.

## Explicit exclusions

- Credentials and authentication material.
- Identity-document images and account-recovery artifacts.
- Unapproved raw WhatsApp, email, or private-chat archives.
- Sensitive traits, beliefs, or positions inferred without explicit confirmation.
