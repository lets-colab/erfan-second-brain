## Separate system-maturity view

The Second Brain should not use the personal-readiness score as a substitute for system certification.

Current evidence supports:
- strong architecture/governance design;
- strong repository coherence;
- executable repository verification with a real passing CI run;
- a current deterministic structural knowledge index;
- live GitHub and Google Drive retrieval;
- partial Notion retrieval with a surfaced permission gap;
- one real failure -> root-cause repair -> successful rerun cycle.

It does **not** yet support production certification because critical runtime, isolation, repeatability, injection-resistance, recovery and confidentiality gates remain unpassed or blocked. See `evaluations/acceptance-evidence.yaml`.

## Change reporting rule

When durable knowledge is added, append to `reviews/knowledge-change-log.md` or a dated review artifact that is linked from the canonical audit trail:

- what was learned;
- source and provenance;
- classification: verified fact, user-stated position, or inference;
- previous overall score;
- new overall score;
- delta and dimensions affected;
- confidence and remaining uncertainty.

Notify Erfan in chat after each meaningful knowledge update. Trivial metadata changes may be grouped.
