When durable knowledge is added, append to `reviews/knowledge-change-log.md`:

- what was learned;
- source and provenance;
- classification: verified fact, user-stated position, or inference;
- previous overall score;
- new overall score;
- delta and dimensions affected;
- confidence and remaining uncertainty.

Notify Erfan in chat after each meaningful knowledge update. Trivial metadata changes
may be grouped, but must still appear in the ledger.
